LupaX Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

LupaX is a Chrome extension for capturing and inspecting web pages. Everything it captures or produces stays on your device. LupaX has no servers and no accounts, and it does not collect analytics, telemetry or crash reports. Nothing you capture is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Network requests

LupaX makes network requests only to download a page's own assets (images, SVGs and fonts) from the sites that host them, when you view or export them in the Asset Explorer. These requests are sent without your cookies. Exporting assets from other sites asks for your permission first, and that access is removed when the export ends. LupaX loads no remote code.

Microphone, camera and screen

LupaX uses the microphone and camera only during a recording you start with those options turned on, and screen or window capture only after you choose what to share in Chrome's picker. They are released as soon as the recording or screenshot ends.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

History keeps your latest 20 recordings and 60 other items (screenshots and exports); older items are removed automatically. You can delete any item from the History page, or everything from Settings, at any time. Uninstalling LupaX deletes its history and settings. Files you downloaded stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them.

Sharing and sale of data

LupaX does not sell or transfer your data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. Its use of data is limited to providing the features described here, in line with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy
