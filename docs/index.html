01 · Inspect

Hover anything. Read its CSS.

Turn on Inspect Mode and point at any element to see its font, size, colors, box model and contrast. Pin it, walk up and down the tree with ↑ ↓, then Copy CSS.

The popup also reads the whole page: its color palette, type scale, spacing, radii, shadows and components.

02 · Capture & record

Any part of the page. Or the whole screen.

Capture the visible area, a region, the full scrolling page or a single element, then mark it up with arrows, text, shapes and blur. Save as PNG, JPG or PDF or copy it to the clipboard.

Record a tab, a window or your desktop with microphone, system audio and a webcam bubble, up to 4K at 60 fps.

Privacy

Your captures stay on your device.

Screenshots, recordings and exports are stored in your browser. LupaX has no servers and no accounts, and it doesn't collect analytics. We never receive what you capture.

LupaX can read a page only after you click its icon or use one of its shortcuts there. Exporting assets from other sites asks first, and that access is removed when the export ends.
