Questions

Is LupaX really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no account, trial or watermark. The code is open source under the Apache-2.0 license.

Does LupaX send my captures anywhere?

No. Captures, recordings and exports stay in your browser and your Downloads folder. LupaX only connects to the sites whose assets you choose to view or export.

What permissions does it ask for, and why?
activeTab
Access to the current tab, only after you click LupaX or use a shortcut there.
scripting
Runs the inspector, page scans and scrolling capture in that tab.
tabCapture
Records the current tab.
desktopCapture
Records or screenshots a window or screen you choose in Chrome's picker.
offscreen
Keeps a tab recording running and prepares files in the background.
downloads
Saves captures, recordings and exports.
clipboardWrite
Copies captures, colors and CSS.
storage, unlimitedStorage
Keeps settings and capture history on your device; recordings can be large.
Optional: other sites
Asked only when you export assets from other sites as a ZIP, and removed when the export ends.
Does it work in other browsers?

LupaX is built and tested for Chrome 116 or later. Other Chromium-based browsers may work, but they aren't tested.

How do I report a bug or a security problem?

Open an issue on GitHub or email support@lupax.app. Please report security problems privately, as the security policy describes.
